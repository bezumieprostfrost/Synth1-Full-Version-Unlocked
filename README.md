## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **16-note Polyphony** — Supports up to 16 simultaneous notes, perfect for complex chords and melodic lines.
- **128 Presets** — Comes with a variety of presets ready for use, allowing quick access to diverse sounds.
- **2-Oscillator Design** — Features two oscillators for rich sound creation, including ring modulation and FM modulation.
- **4 Filter Types** — Provides four distinct filter options to shape your sound, enhancing versatility in sound design.
- **LFO Synchronization** — Includes up to two LFOs that can be synchronized with the sequencer for dynamic modulation effects.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Automated Patterns with 'Arupeije'** — Create intricate automated patterns effortlessly, enhancing your workflow.
- **Built-in Effects** — Offers various effects like delay, chorus, and flanger to further modulate your sound.
- **Legato and Portamento Modes** — Allows for expressive performance techniques that enhance musicality.
- **Parameter Automation** — Users can automate multiple parameters for complex sound manipulation, improving production efficiency.
